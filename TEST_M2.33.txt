Schedule I Arcade M2.33 - Rigid Cabinets + Double Door + Direct WAV Audio

Fixes
- Pac-Man/arcade cabinet and every other prop keep their original mesh proportions. Only the architectural environment shell is deformed to the real five-sided building footprint. Props are repositioned by a rigid center offset instead of per-vertex warping.
- Removed UnityWebRequestMultimedia/DownloadHandlerAudioClip completely. MelonLoader 0.7.3 throws MissingMethodException for that wrapper in this Schedule I build. The packaged 16-bit stereo PCM WAV is now decoded directly and loaded with AudioClip.Create + SetData.
- Old east-side single-door area is covered.
- Added a real visible two-leaf/double-door entrance centered in the shortened north wall.
- Raw arcade room rotates 135 degrees on entry so local inward direction from that door points north-west in world space. Player teleport yaw is left untouched, preserving the NW facing direction.
- Existing five-sided checkerboard floor, hidden placeholder vending/crane props, build grid, cabinet gameplay, and exit flow are preserved.
