SCHEDULE I ARCADE M2.60 - SMOOTH PINBALL STOPPERS

1. Restart Schedule I after installing this build and confirm all four pinball screens remain upright and aligned to their glass.
2. Launch a ball, then hit each bumper, target bank, sling, and the small centre drain guard several times.
3. Confirm each collision returns the ball with a smooth, controlled pace. It must not build into a fast jittery or uncontrollable state after repeated stopper hits.
4. Confirm launch and active-flipper shots still have enough energy to reach the upper playfield.
5. Confirm a central lower return is guarded while both side lanes remain normal drains.
