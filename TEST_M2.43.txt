Schedule I Arcade M2.43 - TV modal, native placement grid, and layout corrections

Expected startup/log state
1. Confirm MelonLoader reports version 0.2.43-tv-grid-layout-fixes.
2. Enter the arcade and check for:
   - Pre-placed interactive Arcade TV flush against north wall: Pong + Snake
   - Arcade floor building enabled using native owned Grid ... perimeterAdded=...
   - Custom arcade furniture initialized: 9 movable cabinet/machine asset(s)
3. There must be no repeating "Method unstripping failed" / OnGUI errors in Latest.log.

Custom machine pickup regression
1. Aim at the Arcade TV and press F. Keep it held for at least 10 seconds.
2. Confirm the furniture HUD/ghost remain responsive and Latest.log stays free of repeated errors.
3. Right-click/Escape to cancel, then repeat with an upright cabinet and a pinball machine.

Interactive TV modal
1. Stand in front of the custom TV. Its attract/menu text must read normally, not backwards.
2. Press E. Confirm the camera moves in to frame the TV screen, the hotbar is hidden, and the
   player cannot walk or look away.
3. Press Tab: the phone must not open; the TV returns to its Pong/Snake menu instead.
4. Try number keys and ordinary game hotkeys: they must not change the hotbar or trigger gameplay.
5. Use arrows + Enter to select Pong/Snake. Confirm arrows operate the TV game.
6. Press Escape. Confirm camera, movement/look, phone access and hotbar return, and the TV restores
   its attract screen.

Vanilla furniture grid
1. Select a normal floor-standing vanilla item, such as the game TV, while inside the arcade.
2. Move its ghost over centre floor, then against each straight wall and the chamfer wall.
3. On every native arcade tile the ghost must become placeable (not permanently red) and it must
   place using the normal vanilla flow.
4. Confirm an item placed on the arcade grid remains visible after walking around and after leaving
   and re-entering the arcade.

Layout / visual pass
1. All four pinball backs are flush toward the west wall; their play fields face into the room.
2. Both stand-up cabinet screens and their helper display quads face the player from the south row.
3. The Street Fighter mural remains normally oriented. Its physical door is on the right/south end
   of that wall, without covering a window or introducing a hole.
4. Ceiling has no long hanging fluorescent bars. The checkerboard floor, wall/roof surfaces, trim,
   and five-sided collision shell remain continuous with no visible gaps.
