Schedule I Arcade M1.8 PERFORMANCE FIX

Fixes the severe stutter seen in M1.6.

- Removed recurring scene-wide StaticDoor scans.
- Removed per-frame scene-wide NPCSummonMenu scans.
- Door discovery now happens only when the player presses E near/at a door.
- Nearby fallback uses Physics.OverlapSphere and only inspects nearby collider parents.
- NPCSummonMenu lookup is targeted and one-shot, with one retry on actual door interaction.
- Missing AssetBundle is checked/logged once instead of twice.
- DebugLogs now defaults false for fresh configs. Existing MelonPreferences may retain your previous value.

Expected idle behavior: no repeated 'Could not identify vanilla Arcade StaticDoor' messages and no recurring global scans.
