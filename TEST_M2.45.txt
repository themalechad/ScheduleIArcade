SCHEDULE I ARCADE M2.45 - PROMPTS, SCREENS AND LIGHTING

1. Restart Schedule I and enter the custom arcade.
2. From more than 2.35m away, or while looking away from a machine, confirm no arcade prompt appears.
3. Move close and aim directly at the Arcade TV, Pac-Man table, or either stand-up cabinet. Confirm
   one high-contrast card appears with its name, `E` play action, and `F` move reminder.
4. Aim directly at a pinball machine or air-hockey table. Confirm only its move card appears.
5. Pick up any machine. Confirm the cards show the held item, a clear green/red placement result,
   and Q/E, click, right-click/Escape controls without any GUI error spam.
6. Inspect the idle TV, both stand-up cabinet screens, and Pac-Man tabletop. They must show bright,
   readable attract displays rather than black rectangles. Press E on each playable screen and
   confirm its game still starts and its idle display returns after Escape.
7. Confirm the room is clearly lit on the floor, pinball row, cabinet row, Pac-Man table and door
   wall, with no long ceiling light bars visible.
8. Start Pac-Man. Confirm its player, ghosts, pellets and energizers use the supplied game art,
   then verify the TV view keeps the complete TV screen and console comfortably in frame.
