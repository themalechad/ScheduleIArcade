SCHEDULE I ARCADE 0.2.0 - VANILLA DOOR EVENT HOOK

Why M1.9 did not show Enter Arcade:
- It watched Input.GetKeyDown(E) and then guessed which StaticDoor the player meant.
- Schedule I's interaction system can invoke door interactions through its own InteractableObject/input path,
  so a real knock can occur without our raw E check seeing it.

0.2.0 changes:
- Harmony hooks the game's actual StaticDoor.Interacted callback.
- Also hooks Building.Doors.DoorKnocker.Knock as a compatibility path.
- No global scene scans and no recurring door scans.
- When the real door callback fires, the exact interacted door is captured.
- If NPCSummonMenu opens, Enter Arcade is cloned into that native menu.
- If no NPC menu appears after 0.45 seconds, a small Enter Arcade / Cancel choice appears.
- Exterior return position is captured from the player's exact location at the door.

Expected log lines after knocking on the Arcade door:
  Installed 2 event-driven vanilla Arcade door interaction hook(s).
  Bound vanilla Arcade entrance: ...
  Vanilla Arcade door interaction detected; preparing Enter Arcade choice.

If the current game build only exposes DoorKnocker:
  Arcade door knock detected; showing Enter Arcade choice.
