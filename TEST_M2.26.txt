Schedule I Arcade M2.26 - native Grid hierarchy placement fix

WHY M2.25 STILL CRASHED
Schedule I GridItem.InitializeGridItem uses GetProperty(grid.transform).PropertyCode.
M2.25 correctly populated Grid.ParentProperty during Awake, but then reparented the Grid
under the synthetic arcade room. The cached property still looked valid in logs, while the
vanilla hierarchy lookup returned null during final placement.

M2.26 FIX
- Keeps the arcade Grid proxy parented under the real owned Property permanently.
- Moves the proxy into the arcade using world position/rotation only.
- Rebinds cloned Tile.OwnerGrid references to the cloned Grid.
- Refreshes native coordinate lookup/size data after positioning.
- Keeps wall Surface proxies under the owned Property hierarchy as well.

4. Place it.
5. Placement should complete without GridItem.InitializeGridItem NullReferenceException.
6. Confirm the placed TV stays in the arcade and can be interacted with.
