SCHEDULE I ARCADE M2.52 - SPACE CADET PINBALL

1. Enter the custom arcade and walk to the row of four pinball machines.
2. Confirm each player-facing backbox display shows SPACE CADET / PRESS E instead of a black rectangle.
3. Aim directly at a machine and press E. The view should frame the pinball display and lock player/hotbar controls.
4. Press SPACE or UP to launch the ball.
5. Use LEFT ARROW or Z for the left flipper; use RIGHT ARROW, SLASH, or X for the right flipper.
6. Confirm bumpers, slings, and lit side targets move the ball and increase the score.
7. Complete both target banks to increase the score multiplier. Drain all balls, then press SPACE to restart.
8. Press ESC to exit. Confirm the player returns to normal arcade movement and the machine stays movable with F.
