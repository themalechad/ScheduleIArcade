Schedule I Arcade M2.10 - room geometry / floor / texture clarity fix

Changes from M2.8:
- closes the two FBX sides that were open to the sky (west and north) with collidable walls
- wall fill uses a clean slice of the original Enviorment_Texture.jpg atlas
- removes the fake back-room exit door; ARCADE_EXIT is now aligned to the real gray door in the FBX (+X wall, z ~= 2.335)
- spawn is just inside that real visible door
- entry/exit/safety recovery now use PlayerMovement.Teleport(position, true) so the CharacterController feet are aligned to the floor
- thick invisible safety floor sits immediately under the real visible floor
- safety recovery checks every 0.05s and catches a drop at 0.75m instead of waiting 4m
- runtime textures remain at their original source resolution (environment 1024x1024, most props 512x512), now with trilinear filtering and 16x anisotropy
- reduced over-bright point lighting from 4.5 to 1.2 intensity so texture detail is not blown out
- isolated room offset reduced from 2500m to 1000m for better precision

Important: the asset pack itself is 512/1024 resolution. M2.10 preserves the original files without downscaling, but true HD detail would require higher-resolution replacement textures.
