Schedule I Arcade M2.28 - Dedicated Arcade Property/Grid Identity
===============================================================

WHY M2.27 TVS VANISHED
----------------------
M2.27 already gave the proxy Grid a unique GUID, but it still used Hyland Manor as the
Grid's ParentProperty. Schedule I's BuildableItem initialization inherits the ParentProperty's
culling state. Because the custom arcade pocket is far from Hyland Manor, the newly placed TV
could immediately inherit Hyland Manor's culled state and all of its renderers were disabled.
The root GameObject still existed, which is why simply SetActive(true) did not bring it back.

WHAT M2.28 CHANGES
------------------
* Adds a dedicated lightweight Schedule I Property identity for the custom arcade.
* PropertyName: Schedule I Arcade
* PropertyCode: scheduleiarcade
* Dedicated stable floor Grid GUID: e8fa16cf-fa2a-4c20-a38f-e571fd1aa5e0
* Dedicated arcade Property has content culling disabled.
* Dedicated Property owns its own PropertyContentsContainer inside the arcade room.
* Vanilla GridItem network initialization can now parent furniture to the arcade container naturally.
* Hyland Manor/other owned properties are used ONLY as a one-time source for native Tile/Grid layout.
  Their PropertyCode, culling state, container, and Grid GUID are NOT reused.
* Wall Surface proxies also use the dedicated arcade Property and stable GUIDs.
* The old forced reparent-every-frame behavior is removed.
* A one-shot CreateGridItem postfix still forces SetCulled(false) as a final safety guard and logs
  the placed item's ParentPropertyCode/world position/culling state.

EXPECTED LOGS
-------------
Created dedicated arcade build property 'Schedule I Arcade' code='scheduleiarcade'. It has its own placement identity/container and content culling is disabled.

Arcade floor building enabled with DEDICATED identity: Property='Schedule I Arcade' code='scheduleiarcade', gridGUID=e8fa16cf-fa2a-4c20-a38f-e571fd1aa5e0, ...

Arcade build: placed GridItem 'TV_Built(Clone)' retained on dedicated arcade grid e8fa16cf-fa2a-4c20-a38f-e571fd1aa5e0; ParentPropertyCode='scheduleiarcade', world=(...), culled=False.
