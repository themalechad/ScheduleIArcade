Schedule I Arcade M2.42 - interactive TV and movable arcade machines

Expected startup/log state
1. Confirm MelonLoader reports version 0.2.42-movable-arcade-machines.
2. Enter the arcade and check for:
   - Pre-placed interactive Arcade TV flush against north wall: Pong + Snake
   - Registered arcade cabinet TV -> arcade_tv
   - Custom arcade furniture initialized: 9 movable cabinet/machine asset(s)

Interactive TV
1. Find the dark/cyan custom TV console against the clear north wall.
2. Aim at its screen or body. The HUD should show both [E] Play Arcade TV and [F] Pick Up Arcade TV.
3. Press E. Confirm the TV displays a two-game menu for Pong and Snake.
4. Use Left/Right and Enter (or Space/E) to select a game.
5. Pong: use Up/Down to move the left paddle and verify score/ball/paddles update.
6. Snake: use arrow keys to move, eat food, grow, collide, and use Enter to retry.
7. Press Tab to return to the TV menu and Escape to exit. The attract screen must return.

Movable machine coverage
Press F on each of these nine visible assets:
1. Arcade TV
2. Stand-up Arcade 1
3. Stand-up Arcade 2
4. Pinball Machine 1
5. Pinball Machine 2
6. Pinball Machine 3
7. Pinball Machine 4
8. Pac-Man Cocktail Table
9. Air Hockey Table

For every asset, verify:
- A bottom-center furniture HUD slot displays the correct asset name and quantity x1.
- The original disappears while held and its full model appears as the placement ghost.
- Green means placeable; red appears over another machine, the doorway, or outside the room.
- Q/E rotates 90 degrees, left click places, and right click/Escape restores the previous position.
- Feet/base sit directly on the checkerboard after placement without floating or sinking.
- Bringing the ghost close to south/east/north/west/chamfer walls snaps its footprint flush to the
  finished face without crossing the wall.

Regression checks
1. Move both stand-up cabinets and the TV, then interact with each screen. The cabinet identity and
   game mapping must survive movement.
2. No ghost or held-item HUD may remain after placement, cancellation, or leaving the arcade.
3. Existing vanilla furniture placement grid and wall placement surfaces must still work.
4. Pac-Man, pinballs, air hockey, cabinets, and TV must never be placeable overlapping each other.
