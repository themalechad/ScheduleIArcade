SCHEDULE I ARCADE M2.48 - FLUSH CRTS AND DOOM-THEMED CABINETS

1. Restart Schedule I and enter the arcade. Inspect each stand-up cabinet from the side: its CRT
   must sit in the recessed display plane with only a tiny 2 mm anti-z-fighting offset, not float
   out near the cabinet collision shell.
2. Inspect stand-up arcade 1. Confirm the complete cabinet uses the original orange/bone hellfire
   arcade skin, a `DOOM I` marquee, and a matching orange idle monitor.
3. Inspect stand-up arcade 2. Confirm the complete cabinet uses the original steel/green sequel
   skin, a `DOOM II` marquee, and a matching green idle monitor.
4. Aim at either new CRT and press E. Confirm the normal arcade demo still starts, Escape returns
   to its matching idle screen, and F still picks up and places the complete cabinet normally.
