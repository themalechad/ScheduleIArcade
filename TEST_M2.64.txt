SCHEDULE I ARCADE M2.64 - EMBEDDED ROOM AND GAMEPLAY POLISH

Purpose
-------
Finish the reusable pinball launcher, remove machine collision traps, smooth Pac-Man, seal the
generated entrance, and place the complete custom room inside the real in-game Arcade building.

Test flow
---------
1. Load a save, use the real Arcade entrance, and choose Enter Arcade.
2. Confirm the custom interior appears at world position (-44.7, -2.9, 136.7) with a 180-degree yaw.
   - The room should be contained by the real Arcade building instead of existing in a remote pocket.
   - The floor, walls, ceiling, machines, prompts, and both exits should remain correctly aligned.
3. Look at the inside of the generated double entrance.
   - Confirm the two leaves overlap cleanly behind the narrow centre seal.
   - No bright vertical gap should remain between them.
4. Walk closely around all four pinball machines, including between neighbouring machines.
   - The player should slide around their rounded lower-body collision.
   - Normal movement must never require jumping to escape an invisible box.
5. Start Space Cadet and press Space or Up.
   - The stronger plunger must carry the ball through the full protected barrel and out the high gate.
   - It must not stall or become trapped inside the launcher.
6. Allow or guide a descending ball into the right-side launch barrel.
   - The spring should catch it at the bottom and restore the launch-ready message.
   - Press Space or Up and confirm the same ball can be fired back into play.
7. Start Pac-Man and hold each arrow direction through several corridors and corners.
   - Pac-Man should ease continuously between grid cells just like the ghosts.
   - Tunnel wraps should remain instant rather than sliding across the whole maze.
8. Exit through both supported interior exits and confirm the player returns to the intended exterior doors.
