Schedule I Arcade M2.40 - full wall-to-wall placement grid

1. Enter the arcade and check the log for:
   - version 0.2.40-full-wall-grid
   - perimeterAdded=80
   - coverage approximately 8.0m x 8.0m
   - 6 wall Surface proxies
2. Equip a floor-standing TV/table/cabinet and test placement against:
   - south wall
   - east wall
   - north wall
   - west wall
   - both sides of the chamfer doorway
3. At each wall, verify the object stays green until its back is visually flush with the wall.
4. Verify placed feet sit directly on the checkerboard without a visible gap or floating.
5. Test a true wall-mounted item on every wall face; it should snap flush from near floor height up
   to the crown trim.
6. Confirm the doorway opening remains excluded and no item can be placed through a physical wall.
