- GREEN: pinball machines moved into a row along the north/back wall, left of the door.

Unity editing
- Copy UnityAssetsToCopy/Assets into a Unity project's Assets folder.
- Use Schedule I Arcade > 1 - Prepare Arcade Room.
- Open Assets/ScheduleIArcade/Generated/ArcadeRoom.prefab.
- Red/green/orange/blue editor-only guides show the requested layout zones.
- Move the actual FBX objects and ARCADE_DOUBLE_DOOR_EDIT_ME however you want.
- Keep ARCADE_SPAWN just inside the entrance and ARCADE_EXIT at the inside face of the entrance.
- Use Schedule I Arcade > 2 - Build Windows AssetBundle.
- Copy ArcadeAssetBundleOutput/scheduleiarcade to Mods/ScheduleIArcade/Assets/scheduleiarcade.
